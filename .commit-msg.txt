Diagnose quad: it is the migration, not the content

Breaking quad's two biggest queries down by page settles it. For quad biking dubai,
Google shows the old /quad-bike-rental-dubai/ URL at position 65.9 on 99
impressions, the homepage at 29.9 on 16, and the actual quad pillar not at all. URL
Inspection says that old URL was last crawled on 5 Aug, five days before the site
went live on this domain, so Google has been serving a month-old snapshot of a page
that no longer exists.

Quad was hit hardest because the old quad URL was the strongest page on the old
site, 671 impressions against buggy's 257. It had the most to lose.

Two smaller causes recorded with it: the new slugs dropped the exact phrase people
search, which must NOT be fixed by migrating again because a second URL change
restarts the clock that is already the problem, and the pillar uses rental four
times and hire never while every high-volume quad query carries one or the other.

And the finding that reframes the whole comparison: buggy is not healthier than
quad, it is luckier. buggyrents.com is a brand match for both buggy and rents. The
same on-page weakness reads as position 25 on buggy terms and 65 on quad ones.
